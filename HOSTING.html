Your private connection

The public PWA hosts the interface, not your files. Use the same private or self-hosted HTTPS relay on both devices, then pair and compare safety codes.

  1. Run the open-source relay on hardware you control with Node.js 22 or later.
  2. Allow this frontend's exact HTTPS origin through BRIDGE_ALLOWED_ORIGINS.
  3. Use a private HTTPS reverse proxy or Tailscale Serve. Never replace existing service routes or expose unrelated Mac tools.
  4. Paste the relay HTTPS address in Settings, connect, and pair your devices.

If your browser blocks public-to-private network connections, open the relay's own HTTPS app instead. Both devices must be able to reach it.

Device sharing

iPhone imports with Photos or Files pickers. Android's installed PWA can receive supported shares from other apps. Native Quick Share and AirDrop availability depends on the device.
