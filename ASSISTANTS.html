Siri

After adding a shortcut, say its name to Siri. For Safari's current page, create a share-sheet shortcut receiving URLs, URL-encode Shortcut Input, and open https://beaudown.github.io/pocket-bridge/#compose=link&content=[encoded input].

Android

Install Pocket Bridge, then use supported system Share menus. Text and Link app shortcuts open drafts; Gemini voice launching depends on your device's assistant integration.

The app cannot inspect other apps' last tabs. Clipboard access requires permission or system Paste. Nothing is automatically transmitted.
